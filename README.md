# Bronois

Deep, soothing stereo brown noise for focus and sleep, right inside Obsidian.

## Features

- **Stereo brown noise:** independent left and right channels for a wide, natural sound. Best on headphones.
- **Shape the sound:** a low-pass filter to make it deeper or brighter, and a bass boost.
- **Sleep timer:** 15 minutes, 30 minutes, 1 hour, 4 hours or 8 hours, with a gentle fade-out at the end.
- **Gentle starts and stops:** fades in and out instead of cutting abruptly.
- **Out of the way:** a play button in the ribbon, an optional status bar control, and a panel with all the settings.
- **Commands:** toggle brown noise, open the Bronois panel, volume up and down, play with a sleep timer, and cancel the sleep timer. Set your own hotkeys in **Settings → Hotkeys**.

## How to use

Click the Bronois icon in the left ribbon to start or stop the noise. Run **Bronois: Open Bronois panel** for the volume, filter, bass boost and sleep timer.

## Privacy

Bronois makes the sound on your computer. It uses no network connection, no recordings and no files outside its own settings.

## Install

From Obsidian: **Settings → Community plugins → Browse**, search for **Bronois**, then **Install** and **Enable**.
